ONE renderer for an address snapshot, included by every mail and the invoice.

It exists because four templates and the invoice each wrote their own version against
`city` and `region` — keys `_address_snapshot` (checkout/services/checkout.py:47) does not
produce. It writes `area` and `state`. So every one of them printed the street line, a
blank line, and nothing else: no recipient, no city, no state, no phone. A courier could
not have delivered from the shipped email, and the invoice carried the same hole.

Takes `address` (a snapshot dict). Every line is conditional, because the snapshot is a
frozen copy of whatever the customer typed and international addresses legitimately skip
postcodes and states.
