The sign-off on every CUSTOMER-facing text part. Included rather than typed out fifteen
times so the social accounts have one home (`apps/notifications/branding.py`) and adding
a platform reaches the text and HTML parts together.

THE TEXT PART IS NOT A FORMALITY. Every message here is multipart/alternative, and the
plain part is what Outlook's reading pane shows on a "plain text" account, what a
screen reader may read, and what spam filters score when the HTML part looks thin. A
message whose text part is a stub scores worse than one with none at all.

The contact address is here as well as in the HTML footer, and it is the SAME one
`send_email` sets as Reply-To — a plain-text reader who cannot click the mailto still has
somewhere to write to, and it is the inbox the reply button reaches anyway.

`ljust` pads the labels into a column so the URLs line up in a monospaced reader.
